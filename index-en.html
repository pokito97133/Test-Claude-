About

Passionate about Saint-Barth's nature

Born from a love of this island and its lush vegetation, Les Jardiniers is a team of 6 passionate professionals, united by a shared vision: excellence in the service of your green spaces.

With over 15 years of experience on the island of Saint-Barthélemy, we have accompanied hundreds of homeowners, villas and prestigious establishments in the creation and maintenance of their tropical gardens.

Our approach combines elegance and professionalism — every intervention is crafted like a work of art, where precision of touch meets refinement of result.

The privacy and intimacy of our clients are at the heart of our commitment. We work with professionalism and confidentiality on every property entrusted to us.
